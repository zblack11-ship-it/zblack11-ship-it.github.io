STICKER ARCHIVE

Just a girl’s sticker collection

I originally intended to upload personal photographs of my roommate’s sticker book to create a visual archive of their sticker collection. However, I experienced difficulties uploading the photographs to the website. Instead of including the original images, I decided to describe each sticker individually and pair each description with an open-access image link. This alternative approach allows me to continue documenting the collection while providing visual references for each sticker. Although the website will not feature the original photographs, it will still serve as a digital archive that highlights the variety, design, and personal character of the collection.

Stickers and Global Waste

Stickers and Global Waste

Although stickers are small everyday objects, their production and disposal can contribute to environmental waste. Many stickers are made with vinyl, plastic coatings, synthetic adhesives, and other materials that make them difficult to recycle. Because stickers are often discarded with the objects they are attached to, they can become part of a much larger plastic waste problem. Understanding the materials used to create stickers encourages us to think about more sustainable ways of collecting, producing, and using them.

Global Waste: Facts to Consider

  • Only 9% of all plastic waste ever produced has been recycled.
  • Approximately 430 million tonnes of plastic are produced globally each year.
  • Stickers and glossy adhesive labels are generally not accepted in paper recycling.
